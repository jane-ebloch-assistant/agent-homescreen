FAQ

Common questions

My CPA handles my taxes. Why do I need this?

Your CPA is great at filing. But have they ever called you in October to say "convert $18K to your Roth before year-end"? Filing and planning are different jobs. We plan, they file.

I use a robo-advisor that does tax-loss harvesting.

TLH is one of ~10 strategies. Your robo doesn't know your W-2 income, your spouse's 401k, your ISO exercise schedule, or that you're 2 years from IRMAA. We see the whole picture.

How is this different from TurboTax?

TurboTax helps you fill out forms in April. We tell you what to do in March, June, September, and November so that when April comes, your bill is already lower.

Do you move my money or file my taxes?

No. We use read-only access to analyze your accounts. You stay in control. We show you the math, you decide. Think of us as a tax-smart second opinion that watches 24/7.

I don't have enough money for this to matter.

The biggest ROI on tax planning is actually in the $250K–$1M range. $5K in annual savings is 1–2% of your portfolio. That compounds. Over 20 years, it's huge.

Is my data secure?

Bank-level encryption (AES-256), SOC 2 Type II certified, and we use Plaid for account connections. We can't move money even if we wanted to.
